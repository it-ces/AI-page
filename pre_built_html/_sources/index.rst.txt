Iván Trujillo Abella
====================

Economist, M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence, and currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Mathematics at the Pontificia Universidad Javeriana. His interests include optimization, finance,  machine learning and quantitative methods for decision-making.

**Profiles**

- `GitHub <https://github.com/it-ces>`_
- `LinkedIn <https://www.linkedin.com/in/iv%C3%A1n-trujillo-abella-1a683419a/>`_


This microsite presents educational materials, research projects, and teaching resources in optimization,  statistics, finance, economics, data science, and artificial intelligence, with practical implementations in Python.


Areas of specialization
-----------------------

- Artificial intelligence
- Metaheuristic Optimization (Genetic Algorithms, Grey Wolf Optimizer, Ant Colony, ...)  
- Financial Optimization
- Credit Risk Modeling
- Prescriptive analytics
